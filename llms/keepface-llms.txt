# Keepface

> Keepface is an influencer marketing platform. Brands discover creators, run
> outreach and campaigns, and read AI-graded intelligence on any Instagram or
> TikTok profile. Public marketplace, public creator profiles, and a full
> suite of brand-side products live at https://keepface.com.
> The site is available in 8 languages: English (default), Azerbaijani,
> Turkish, Russian, Romanian, Greek, Georgian, Chinese. Use /az/, /tr/,
> /ru/, /ro/, /el/, /ge/, /zh/ prefixes to fetch a translated variant.

## What Keepface does (one-line answers)
- Find creators that match your brief, by category, country, audience, or list.
- Send personalized outreach to hundreds of creators in one workflow.
- Pay creators via escrow, in Keepcoin (KPC) or local currency.
- Track delivery, posts, and revenue with on-platform analytics.
- Unlock the AI Intelligence Profile on any creator for a 20-section read.
- Run brand-level affiliate programs at 20% commission, opt-out anytime.

## AI Influencer Intelligence
- [AI Intelligence — overview](https://keepface.com/intelligence): Decode any influencer with AI. The engine reads 4,000+ data points per profile (audience quality, brand safety, real pricing power, campaign fit) and produces a 20-section Intelligence Profile. Browse the Top 100 creators or analyze any Instagram/TikTok handle on demand. Member unlock $25, guest unlock $35.

## Products
- [Affiliate](https://keepface.com/products/affiliate): Brand-level affiliate program. Always-on, 20% commission, opt-out toggle. Excel monthly imports for Trendyol-pattern sales feeds. Ghost link-back for non-Keepface creators.
- [AI Analysis](https://keepface.com/products/ai-analysis): Auto-grade posts, audiences, and campaign delivery against brand criteria.
- [AI Intelligence Profile](https://keepface.com/products/ai-intelligence-profile): Full 20-section deep read on any creator handle (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube).
- [CRM](https://keepface.com/products/crm): Creator CRM with lists, owner emails, multi-channel inbox.
- [Customer Advocacy](https://keepface.com/products/customer-advocacy): Turn buyers into creators — reviews, NPS, coupons, referrals, UGC rights, leaderboards, consents.
- [Employee Advocacy](https://keepface.com/products/employee-advocacy): Internal post-library + employee distribution for organic reach.
- [Kee — AI agent](https://keepface.com/products/kee-agent): Conversational brand assistant that runs marketplace ops via chat.
- [Reporting](https://keepface.com/products/reporting): On-platform analytics + shareable campaign report links.
